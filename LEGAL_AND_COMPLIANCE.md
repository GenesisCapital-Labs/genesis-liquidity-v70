# Genesis100: Legal Identity & Regulatory Framework

## Corporate Structuring
Genesis100 operates as a decentralized technological infrastructure. The protocol and its accompanying user interfaces are developed, maintained, and licensed by **Genesis Capital Web3 SaaS**.

To ensure absolute adherence to global decentralized finance (DeFi) standards, Genesis Capital operates under a **Decentralized Autonomous Organization (DAO) Wrapper** legal structure, formally established in the **British Virgin Islands (BVI)**.

*   **Operating Entity:** Genesis Capital Web3 SaaS (BVI DAO Entity)
*   **Jurisdiction of Code:** British Virgin Islands (BVI)
*   **Entity Classification:** Virtual Asset Software Provider (Non-Custodial)

## Regulatory Authorization (VASP Status)
Genesis Capital is classified strictly as a software provider and non-custodial smart contract routing engine. 
*   **No Fiduciary Custody:** The entity does not hold, pool, or manage fiat currency or retail crypto-assets on behalf of users. 
*   **MiCA Alignment:** The protocol’s decentralized liquidity mechanisms are architected to align proactively with the Markets in Crypto-Assets (MiCA) transparency and algorithmic execution frameworks. 
*   **VASP Exemption:** Because the Genesis100 V-70 Router relies on immutable, user-executed smart contracts rather than centralized ledger management, it operates outside the scope of traditional brokerage licensing, functioning instead as an authorized software licensing entity (SaaS) under BVI digital asset guidelines.

## Dispute Resolution & Accountability
Unlike traditional opaque financial entities that force biased corporate arbitration, Genesis100 utilizes **Decentralized SLA Protocols**.
Disputes regarding algorithmic execution are verifiable on-chain via the Binance Smart Chain (BSC) public ledger. The immutable nature of the smart contracts ensures that yield distribution (Maker/Taker fees) and liquidity deployment are executed exactly as coded, removing human counterparty risk and negating the need for centralized legal arbitration.

## Independent Audits
The corporate entity submits its open-source smart contracts to continuous cryptographic audits to ensure principal protection mechanisms function as mathematically defined. (See `audit-report-2026.pdf` in the main documentation portal).
